小K提词器隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日 · 适用于小K提词器 / KPrompt 2.3

隐私摘要

小K提词器不要求注册账户，不投放广告，不使用第三方分析或跟踪服务，也不会将你的文案、录音或视频上传到开发者服务器。

文案内容

你创建、粘贴或导入的提词文案及其排版设置保存在当前设备。导入文件时只读取你选择的文件，分享文案时由你选择接收位置。删除文案会移除该文案的本地记录；系统备份依照你的 iCloud 设置处理。

麦克风

只有在你主动使用声控跟读或内置拍摄时才会使用。声控跟读仅在本机分析声音活动，不识别、不转写、不保存、不上传说话内容；拍摄声音只写入你主动录制的视频。

相机与照片

内置拍摄需要相机和麦克风权限，用于你主动开启的预览和有声录像，不会在后台拍摄。录制片段在当前设备临时保存，完成后合并并添加到系统相册。未开始保存时退出拍摄页面，会丢弃未保存的片段。照片权限仅用于添加完成的视频，不读取或扫描你的其他照片和视频。

字体资源与购买

首次选择宋体或圆体时，iOS 可能需要从 Apple 下载字体资源；下载期间使用标准字体，准备完成后自动应用。购买、价格获取与恢复购买通过 Apple App Store 处理，小K不会读取你的付款信息。

购买权限缓存

已在本机验证的正式永久购买和原付费下载权限保存在系统 Keychain，用于重启或断网时恢复使用。缓存包含商店验证凭证及撤销状态，不含文案、音视频或支付卡信息；不通过 iCloud Keychain 同步，也不随设备备份转移到新设备。Keychain 记录在卸载后可能保留；换机需通过 App Store 重新确认购买权限。购买与恢复购买需要联网，已验证的退款或撤销会更新本机权限。

浮窗与控制中心

浮窗使用 iOS 画中画能力，小K无法读取其他 App 的页面或操作。控制中心按钮只负责启动小K，不收集使用记录。

权限与数据删除

你可以在系统设置中关闭相机、麦克风或照片权限，也可以在 App 内删除文案。卸载 App 会移除其应用沙盒内的文案、设置和临时文件，但本机 Keychain 中的购买权限缓存可能保留。已保存到相册或分享出去的内容需要在对应位置自行删除；系统备份依照你的设置处理。

数据共享与儿童隐私

我们不出售或共享个人信息，也不跨 App 或网站跟踪用户。小K不以收集儿童个人信息为目的，并且不提供账户系统或开发者数据服务器。

隐私问题联系

有关本政策或隐私处理的问题，可联系 kirala2026@163.com。请勿发送私密文案、录音、视频或付款信息。

KPrompt Privacy Policy

Updated October 4, 2026 · Applies to KPrompt 2.3

Privacy at a glance

KPrompt does not require an account, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking services. Your scripts, audio, and videos are never uploaded to developer servers.

Script content

Scripts you create, paste or import, along with their typography settings, are stored on this device. When importing, KPrompt only reads the file you select. You choose where a shared script is sent. Deleting a script removes its local record; system backups follow your iCloud settings.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you start voice pacing or camera recording. Voice pacing analyzes sound activity on your device. It does not recognize, transcribe, save, or upload what you say. Camera audio is included only in videos you choose to record.

Camera and Photos

In-app recording requires camera and microphone access for the preview and video with sound that you choose to start. KPrompt does not record in the background. Clips are temporarily stored on this device, then merged and added to Photos when you finish. Leaving the recording screen before saving starts discards unsaved clips. Photos access is only used to add the completed video, not to read or scan your other photos or videos.

Font Resources and Purchases

When you first select Songti or Yuanti, iOS may need to download font resources from Apple. The system font is used during the download, and the selected font is applied once ready. Purchases, pricing and purchase restoration are handled by the Apple App Store. KPrompt does not read your payment information.

Purchase Access Cache

Production lifetime purchases and previous paid-download access verified on this device are saved in the system Keychain so access can be restored after a restart or while offline. The cache includes store-verified evidence and revocation status, not scripts, audio, video or payment card information. It does not sync through iCloud Keychain or transfer to a new device through backups. Keychain records may remain after uninstalling. A new device must confirm access again through the App Store. Purchasing and restoring purchases require an internet connection. Verified refunds or revocations update access on this device.

Floating prompt and Control Center

The floating prompt uses iOS Picture in Picture. KPrompt cannot read other apps’ screens or actions. The Control Center button only launches KPrompt and does not collect usage records.

Permissions and deleting data

You can turn off camera, microphone or Photos access in system Settings and delete scripts within the app. Uninstalling removes scripts, settings and temporary files from the app’s sandbox, but purchase access cached in this device’s Keychain may remain. Content saved to Photos or shared elsewhere must be deleted at those locations. System backups follow your settings.

Data sharing and children’s privacy

We do not sell or share personal information, or track users across apps or websites. KPrompt is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not provide an account system or developer data servers.

Privacy enquiries

For questions about this policy or privacy handling, contact kirala2026@163.com. Do not send private scripts, audio, videos or payment information.
