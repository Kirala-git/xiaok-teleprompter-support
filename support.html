KPROMPT SUPPORT

小K提词器使用帮助

适用于小K提词器 / KPrompt 2.3 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日。建议使用最新版本的 iOS 和 App。

快速排查

  1. 在后台彻底关闭小K，再重新打开。
  2. 检查相机、麦克风和照片权限。
  3. 重启 iPhone 并确认有足够存储空间。
  4. 更新到 App Store 中的最新版本。

常见问题

如何切换语言？

在“关于小K → 设置 → 语言”中选择跟随系统、简体中文或 English，界面立即切换并记住选择，不翻译或改写文案。系统权限提示和控制中心按钮遵循 iOS 的语言设置。

如何切换深色和浅色外观？

在“关于小K → 设置 → 外观”中选择跟随系统、浅色或深色。整个 App 立即应用并保存选择；跟随系统按 iPhone 的设置自动切换。提词文字色、底色与透明度仍在提词设置中单独调整。

声控跟读没有反应怎么办？

声控跟读仅在全屏提词中提供，根据设备本地的声音活动推进，不识别、转写或匹配所说的文字。请允许麦克风权限，在安静环境中调整跟读灵敏度；其他 App 占用麦克风时会暂时匀速推进。

浮窗如何开始或暂停？

先在悬浮准备页查看预览，再点击“开始悬浮”。轻点系统画中画窗口显示播放或暂停控制，暂停保留当前进度。请在真机使用，并确认 iOS 画中画已启用；窗口的大小和位置由系统管理。

拍摄视频如何保存？

内置拍摄需要相机和麦克风权限。录制可暂停并继续，结束并保存后合并为一段视频。请允许向照片图库添加内容并检查剩余空间，等待保存完成再退出。保存开始前退出会丢弃未保存片段。

如何调整排版与英文阅读？

在文案编辑页打开“排版”，分别保存每篇文案的字体、字号、行距、段距、字距和每行最多字数。宋体或圆体首次使用可能需要从 Apple 下载字体。中文按字、英文按整词推进与高亮；英文速度为词/秒，中英混排仍保持英文词完整。

横屏设置和小相机预览如何使用？

镜像、方向和显示可直接点按切换；字号、节奏、补光与配色打开紧凑面板，横屏保持约半屏浮层。开启屏幕补光后，小相机预览可拖动并保存位置。

断网或重新打开会丢失已购买权限吗？

本机已确认的正式永久购买会保存在系统 Keychain，重启或断网仍可使用；后台获取价格和商店记录不阻塞已确认权限。24 小时试用不会自动扣费或因重启重新计时。购买与恢复购买需联网；新设备请使用原 Apple 账户恢复购买。已验证的退款或撤销会更新权限。

如何从指定位置提词？

暂停后使用上一段、下一段或回到开头控制位置，也可手动选择希望开始的文字位置，再继续提词。

控制中心找不到“开始提词”怎么办？

该功能需要 iOS 18 或更高版本。先打开一次小K，再进入控制中心编辑界面，搜索“小K提词器”或“开始提词”并添加。

KPrompt Support

For KPrompt 2.3 · Updated October 4, 2026. Use the latest iOS and app version.

Quick checks

  1. Fully close KPrompt and reopen it.
  2. Check camera, microphone and Photos permissions.
  3. Restart your iPhone and check available storage.
  4. Update to the latest App Store version.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language?

Choose Follow System, 简体中文 or English under About KPrompt → Settings → Language. The interface changes immediately and remembers your choice without translating or rewriting your scripts. System permission prompts and Control Center buttons follow the iOS language setting.

How do I switch between light and dark appearance?

Choose Follow System, Light or Dark under About KPrompt → Settings → Appearance. The entire app changes immediately and remembers your choice. Follow System uses your iPhone’s appearance setting. Prompt text color, background color and opacity are adjusted separately in prompting settings.

What if voice pacing does not respond?

Voice pacing is available in fullscreen prompting and advances based on sound activity on your device. It does not recognize, transcribe or match your words. Allow microphone access and adjust sensitivity in a quiet place. If another app uses the microphone, prompting temporarily advances at a steady pace.

How do I start or pause the floating prompt?

Check the preview on the preparation screen, then tap Start Floating. Tap the Picture in Picture window to show its play and pause controls. Pausing preserves your position. Use a physical device with iOS Picture in Picture enabled. iOS manages the window’s size and position.

How do I save a recorded video?

In-app recording needs camera and microphone access. You can pause and continue recording; finishing and saving merges the clips into one video. Allow adding to Photos, check available storage and wait for saving to finish before leaving. Leaving before saving starts discards unsaved clips.

How do typography and English prompting work?

Open Typography in the script editor to save font, size, line and paragraph spacing, letter spacing and maximum line length for each script. Songti or Yuanti may need an initial font download from Apple. Chinese advances by character; English advances and highlights complete words using words per second. English words also stay whole in mixed scripts.

How do landscape settings and the small camera preview work?

Tap to toggle mirroring, orientation and display mode. Font size, pace, fill light and colors open compact panels that remain about half the screen width in landscape. When screen fill light is enabled, you can drag the small camera preview and its position is saved.

Will going offline or reopening the app lose purchased access?

Production lifetime access verified on this device is saved in the system Keychain and remains available after a restart or while offline. Background price and store-record loading does not block verified access. The 24-hour trial does not charge automatically or restart when reopening. Purchasing and restoring require internet access. On a new device, restore using the original Apple Account. Verified refunds or revocations update access.

How do I start prompting from a specific position?

Pause and use Previous, Next or Back to Start. You can also manually choose the text position you want, then continue prompting.

What if Start Prompting is missing from Control Center?

This feature needs iOS 18 or later. Open KPrompt once, edit Control Center, then search for KPrompt or Start Prompting and add the control.
